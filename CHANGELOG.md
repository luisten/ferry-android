## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Log in to Instagram on its own web page, inside the app.
- The inbox: every account's chats in one list, newest first, with who wrote last, an unread dot and the time. Each of
  your accounts has its own colour and letter, and the chips at the top show one account's chats at a time.
- Folders, top right: General (business and creator accounts), Requests and Archived. New message requests show as a
  card at the top of the list, with who's waiting but not what they wrote.
- Search your chats by name.
- Open a chat to read it: your messages in your account's colour, replies, reactions, edits, unsent messages, "Seen",
  and in groups who wrote what and who has seen it. Scroll up for older messages. Photos, videos and voice messages
  show as a label for now.
- Write back: send a message, reply to one, react (long press, or double tap for a heart), edit within 15 minutes,
  unsend, copy. Your message shows at once; if it can't be sent, tap it to try again. The other side sees when you're
  typing.
- Message requests: accept, delete, or reply (which accepts). Until you do, they don't see that you've read them.
- Archive a chat (long press, or the ⋯ menu in the chat). It's on this phone only, and a new message brings it back.
- New message: choose which of your accounts it's from, search Instagram for someone and start the chat.
- Stickers and GIFs from Instagram's GIPHY picker are named as such, instead of "a message Ferry can't show".
- A chat that opens unread shows where the new messages start, even when only the newest has arrived.
- Photos, videos, GIFs, stickers and voice messages show in the chat; tap one to see it full screen or play it. Shared
  reels and stories load when you tap them. Photos and videos sent together show as a pile you can open.
- Send photos, videos and GIFs (the + in the message bar), and voice messages (tap the mic, or hold it). Where a photo
  was taken never leaves your phone: location and other details are removed first.
- A welcome screen, and after each login a name, colour and mark for the account, so its chats are easy to tell apart.
- Accounts: see each account's state, rename it or change its colour and mark, log in again when Instagram signs you
  out (a banner and the chat say so), or remove an account from this phone.
- Your login stays on this phone, encrypted, and out of backups.
