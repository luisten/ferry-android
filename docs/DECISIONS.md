# Decisions: Android, iOS 1.7 parity

This logs the Android-side decisions for bringing Ferry Messenger for Android to iOS 1.7 parity (newest at the bottom).
The iOS repo's `docs/DECISIONS.md` and `PLAN-android.md` hold the earlier ones.

## Known limitations and follow-ups

Found by the testers on the demo or noted by the implementers; none was judged a blocker for 1.7. Candidates for separate issues.

- Landscape with the keyboard up, a reply bar and a set bar leaves no room for the message field (A51).
- A pressed bubble's ripple has square corners (the default indication on `MessageMenu`'s `combinedClickable`).
- Icons aren't mirrored in right-to-left languages: the back and reply arrows point the left-to-right way.
- Header and inbox buttons lose their circles in dark mode (the surface is the page's black); the demo's black video cards disappear on it too.
- A busy chat that pages back as it opens still moves the "New messages" line once (A40).
- At font scale 1.3 to 2.0 the Folders badge covers its icon, the thread header's account chip is clipped at 2.0, and the composer's placeholder wraps.
- A slow horizontal drag (300 ms, long) inside a bubble that can't swipe opens its sheet; a slow inbox swipe to about half the row springs back (Material's threshold).
- The screen stack isn't saved: a language change or Android closing the process returns to the inbox (A51); the draft is dropped when leaving a chat (A52).
- `deliver`/`deliverMedia` answer "This is the demo: nothing is sent." for a live chat that has gone from `rooms`; only the demo should say it.
- Not verifiable on the emulator, so open for a real phone: TalkBack's reading (bubble labels, "Reply", link and set-bar actions, day chips as headings), haptics at the swipe threshold, the swipe's feel against gesture navigation, the voice bars' seek against the swipe, the keyboard's own GIF and sticker search, what Gboard does with an image a field refuses (A53), "No camera app on this phone.", process death while the camera is in front, and ordered selection in other phones' photo pickers (Android 8 to 12 fall back to older pickers).
- No General chats in the demo, so that folder's Mark as Read/Unread wasn't tried; "marked unread" surviving a real launch (the demo keeps it in memory) and an account's removal keeping its marks weren't either.
- Needing a live account (PLAN §8, in the private notes): a reaction's log entry arriving live and its removal; the re-login after a resnapshot; Instagram's own camera media kept in a chat, and view-once lines; several photos and videos reaching Instagram in order, then the text, with a retry in the middle; a real camera capture with its location removed in what the other side gets; Gboard's clipboard strip, a keyboard GIF and an Emoji Kitchen sticker, pasting a video; the inbox row refreshing on a live update (fixed by A39 on the demo, which takes the same path).
- Worth telling the iOS side: iOS sends the text at once while its uploads are chained, so its "then your text" may not hold; Android queues the text (A12).
- The signed APK on a friend's phone: Play Protect, Google's developer verification, and a 16 KB-page phone.
- Days on a real phone for battery, phone makers' battery savers, and whether Instagram minds the always-on connection (A8).
